The Tiles

Sixteen rooms: eight in the house, eight out the back. You meet them one draw at a time, which is the whole idea — but here they all are, for when you want to know what you are hoping to turn over.

The plan beside each room shows which walls have a way through. Where a tile ends up pointing is the game's business: it turns each one as it lays it down.
