Original game

This game is based on Zombie in my Pocket (2007), a free print-and-play solo board game designed by Jeremiah Lee. All credit for the original design, mechanics and concept goes to him.

Original on BoardGameGeek

This version

Zombie in My Pocket is an unofficial, fan-made browser version. It reimplements the original's rules and numbers — which aren't copyrightable — under a different title, with its own setting, room and item names, artwork and written text. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original designer.

To be clear about what is whose: the design is Jeremiah Lee's. The name, art and words here are ours.

The original print-and-play is released under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Note that there is also a separate commercial edition of a similarly named game from a different publisher; this version is related to neither.

Sound

Some cues are recorded, from two CC0 packs by Kenney: RPG Audio and Impact Sounds, both released under CC0 1.0. CC0 asks for nothing in return; the credit is here because giving it costs nothing. The rest of the sound is synthesised in the browser, with no files at all.

Every audio file, its source and its licence are logged in assets/audio/CREDITS.txt in the repository.
